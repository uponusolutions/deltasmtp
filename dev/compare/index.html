deltasmtp vs. go-smtp

How deltasmtp compares with emersion/go-smtp. A small (3.5 KB) and a large (13.4 MB) mail are sent to two recipients over one connection reused for every mail, without timeouts or limits. The client of each library sends to its own server, the two libraries are never mixed. Pipelining is used wherever it is supported. The first section uses everything each library supports, the second sends with DATA in both.
