deltasmtp vs. go-smtp

How deltasmtp compares with emersion/go-smtp, each in its fastest configuration: one connection reused for every mail, no timeouts or limits, and CHUNKING and PIPELINING where supported. A small (3.5 KB) and a large (13.4 MB) mail are sent to two recipients. The client of each library sends to its own server, the two libraries are never mixed.
